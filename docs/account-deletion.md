# PHRMS - Account & Data Deletion Request

**App:** PHRMS (Patient Health Records Management System)
**Developer / Operator:** Sassoon General Hospital, Pune, India
**Last updated:** 12 May 2026

## Who can request deletion through this page

PHRMS is a restricted institutional clinical application. Only the
following people can request deletion through this page:

- **Nursing staff** who registered a PHRMS account.
- **Administrators** who hold credentials issued by the hospital.

PHRMS is not used directly by patients. If you are a patient and
want clinical records held about you deleted or corrected, contact
Dr. Mrudula Phule - Sassoon General Hospital at
**phulevaishali@gmail.com** — not this page.

## How to submit a deletion request

1. From the device or email address associated with your PHRMS
   account, send an email to **phulevaishali@gmail.com** with
   the subject line **"PHRMS — Delete my account"**.
2. In the body, include:
   - Your PHRMS **username**.
   - Your **full name** as registered.
   - The **hospital** you are affiliated with.
3. We will reply within **7 working days** to confirm the request
   and verify your identity (we may ask you to confirm a detail
   that only the account holder would know).
4. Once verified, deletion is completed within a further
   **15 days**.

If you do not receive a reply within 7 working days, follow up to
the same address with "Follow-up" in the subject line.

## What gets deleted

When your request is processed, the following data is permanently
removed from our systems:

- Your PHRMS account record (username, hashed password, full name,
  hospital affiliation, account creation date).
- Every Firebase Cloud Messaging push-notification token registered
  by any device on which you signed in. Push notifications stop
  immediately.
- Your authentication sign-in history.

## What is retained, and why

The following data is **not deleted** when your account is removed,
because Indian medical-record retention rules and the hospital's
institutional record-keeping policy require it to be preserved:

- **Patient clinical records** (demographics, baseline assessments,
  periodic outcome assessments, vital signs, audit forms) that you
  entered on behalf of patients while you held the account. These
  records are part of the patient's medical history and belong to
  the patient and the hospital, not to the nurse who recorded them.
- **The fact that an assessment was completed and at what time**
  (i.e. the timestamp), kept inside the patient's clinical record
  for clinical-audit purposes. Your name will be disassociated from
  these timestamps where the law permits.

These retained records are kept for the period mandated by Indian
medical-record retention rules (typically a minimum of three years
for outpatient records and longer for surgical / inpatient
records). They are not used for any purpose other than clinical
care, internal audit, and legal compliance.
